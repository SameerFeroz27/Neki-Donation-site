Neki

Give with trust.

Neki is a donation platform for real, verified campaigns — a widow's monthly ration, a child's surgery, a village hand-pump, a student's fees. Pick a campaign, choose your amount, and pay the way you already pay: Request to Pay through Raast, or credit / debit card. Every rupee is tracked, and each campaign shows you exactly what it still needs.

Each campaign is verified by our field team. Give once, or set up a monthly gift.

About Neki

Donations are held in a dedicated account and released to each campaign in stages, against bills and photos. Our platform fee is 0% — the full amount reaches the campaign. Card details never touch our servers; they are handled by our PCI-DSS compliant payment provider.
